"Waymark" is a fictional train ticket app for phones. This is the results screen after a search, where the person picks a train. As in other ticket apps, the fare type is chosen on the next screen; this one shows each train's Advance price.

Audience and scene: someone booking a trip for tomorrow morning, on their phone on the sofa the evening before. They have to arrive by 10:30 for a meeting and want the cheapest train that gets them there on time without a risky connection. They expect this to take a minute or two.

Business objective: more people book a cheaper Advance fare in the app instead of buying an Anytime ticket at the station, and fewer ask for refunds after missing a tight connection.

What this audience already uses every day: the departure board at the station, and a maps app for journey times.

Products in this category: Trainline, National Rail Enquiries, LNER, Google Maps.

Content (fictional; use exactly, and do not add data):
- Search: Northgate Central to Aldbury, Friday 7 June, 1 adult, no railcard, arrive by 10:30.
- Fare types: Advance is valid on the chosen train only, is non-refundable, and can be changed for a £10 fee. Anytime is valid on any train that day and is refundable. Anytime costs £78.00 on every train below.
- Trains:
  1. 07:12 to 09:05, 1 h 53 min, direct. Advance £34.50, 3 left at this price.
  2. 07:40 to 09:58, 2 h 18 min, 1 change at Fenwick with 12 minutes to change. Advance £27.00.
  3. 08:12 to 10:04, 1 h 52 min, direct. Advance £41.00. Busy: standing is likely.
  4. 08:45 to 10:51, 2 h 06 min, 1 change at Fenwick with 6 minutes to change. Advance £29.50. Arrives after 10:30.
  5. 09:12 to 11:03, 1 h 51 min, direct. Advance £36.00. Arrives after 10:30.
- Actions: choose a train, which opens the fare choice; edit the search; see earlier or later trains.
- Do not invent other trains, promotions, ratings, live delays or loyalty points. No photography is supplied and none may be faked.
- Standard app chrome that carries no data of its own is expected, as on any shipped screen: status and navigation bars, the search summary, earlier and later trains, sort and filter controls, a tab bar.

Representative state for every study: the results as listed, nothing chosen yet.
