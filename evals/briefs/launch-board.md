"Launch Board" is the counter screen for Saltmarsh Kayak, a fictional sea-kayak rental shop. Staff use it on a tablet (landscape, about 1180px wide) standing at the counter in bright daylight, between customers; decisions take a few seconds. The owner also checks it on a phone from home.

Primary task: look at today's conditions and decide which two-hour rental slots to offer, then mark each slot Open or Closed. A closed slot needs a short reason that staff can read back to customers.

Business objective: no slot offered that the shop rule forbids, and no customer turned away from a slot that was fine. Staff are seasonal and get ten minutes of training on this screen.

What these staff already read every day: the printed tide table, the harbour's signal flags, and the departure board at the ferry terminal next door.
